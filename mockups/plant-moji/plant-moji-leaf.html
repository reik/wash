Plant-moji: the leaf is the moji

One leaf, one mark. No pot, no face, no whole plant.

Each plant's moji uses its own leaf outline, so a ginkgo tree, a fern and a cactus look nothing alike. The feeling comes from how the leaf bends, plus a single manga mark.
