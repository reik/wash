# 2026-10-02

- Started from [ぬい日和 Nuibiyori](https://www.nuibiyori.com/) as inspiration: a hobby app where the object (not the user) is the protagonist.
- Goal set: international market from day one.
- Explored blind boxes, plushies, sourdough, beachcombing, then houseplants; competitor searches showed most categories are crowded with catalogue and care tools. See [[competitive-landscape]].
- Houseplant feature list rejected as having no hook; AI letters, avatars, timelapse, AR faces and growth prediction all already exist.
- Plant pen pals proposed; user felt it lacked impact. Parked; the homeroom concept is reused. See [[parking-lot]].
- Two hooks chosen to incubate: [[every-plant-has-a-past]] and [[sprout-day]]. Both added to Backlog with status drafting.
- Next: experiment 1 for Every Plant Has a Past (story call-out); competitor search plus pilot design for Sprout Day.
- Brainstormed "plants that speak". Research: owner-facing talking plants are crowded (Talking Plant, BotanAI with preset personalities, PlantAI and others); plant-to-plant chat and a cross-owner voice were not found.
- Decided: one shared voice for both ideas; "past life" = any period before the current home (4 sources), with newborns as the fallback; AI drafts and the owner sends, edits or keeps quiet; personality earned from real events (species temperament + rule-based traits); no open-ended chat with your own plant. Recorded in [[plant-voice]] (Backlog, drafting).
- New requirements from the user: daily use (a couple of visits a day to see friends' conversations, photos, updates); iPhone + Android apps; AI moderation must stop sexual, violent, discriminatory and hurtful posts before they appear.
- Research: Apple guideline 1.2 requires UGC filtering, reporting, blocking and contact info (plus a zero-tolerance EULA and 24-hour action in practice) and bans random/anonymous chat; Azure Content Safety is trained on Japanese; OpenAI omni-moderation is free (text + images) but Japanese isn't documented. See [[competitive-landscape#Moderation APIs and app store rules]].
- Added [[daily-rhythm]] (time-zone relay, ≤3 lines per plant per day, max 2 push notifications a day), [[content-moderation]] (checked before posting, nothing posts if a check fails, plus a kindness check and a plant-vocabulary test set) and [[tech-direction]] (Expo React Native proposal). The [[plant-voice]] rate limit was raised from about 1 a day to one per part of the day. Homerooms are now mixed across ≥3 time-zone bands instead of "random".
