# P0 — Tone samples

Feel test for [[plant-voice]] and [[windowsill-talk]]. **Revised 2026-10-08 to sound like actual conversation** — questions, asides, interruptions, replies — not caption-style one-liners.

Back to [[roadmap]] (P0) · [[index]]

## How to run this test
1. Read each line in under 3 seconds. Mark ✅ / 😐 / ❌.
2. Key question this round: **does it sound like someone talking?** If it reads like a fortune cookie or a caption, mark 😐 or ❌ even if it's clever.
3. Show 5–10 to a plant-owning friend. Ask: "which would you screenshot?"
4. Pass signal: **you mark ≥70% ✅**, and the friend picks at least 2 to screenshot.
5. Rewrite the ❌ lines once. If a second pass still flops, revisit the personality engine ([[plant-voice]]).

## Public circle talk (voice stage × temperament)

### Newborn (curious, naive)
1. *(first new leaf)* "Oh— something opened. On me?? I came folded??"
2. *(first watering)* "The tall one just poured sky into my pot. Wait. Was that weather?"
3. *(hearing about winter)* "Bjørn, keep talking. What's 'the light is leaving'?? Where does it go?"
4. *(morning sun)* "It's back! The bright wall! It disappears every night and NOBODY talks about it."

### Stoic (snake plant)
5. *(watering after 3 weeks)* "Mm. Water. Thanks."
6. *(heatwave, 37°C)* "Thirty-seven today. Anyway."
7. *(repot)* "New pot. Same me. Moving on."
8. *(reply to a dramatic friend's droop)* "Mara. You're not dying. You're thirsty. Drink something."

### Dramatic (peace lily)
9. *(thirst, then watered)* "I was DYING, everyone saw it— oh. Water. …I meant to do that."
10. *(cold snap)* "Okay who put the window in the freezer?? Hello? Anyone?"
11. *(new leaf)* "Don't all look at once. …Okay, look."
12. *(friend flowered first)* "Lua flowered?? Before ME? …Tell her I said congrats. Don't tell her I said it twice."

### Habitual (prayer plant)
13. *(evening)* "Folding up, goodnight. Yes, same time as yesterday. That's the point."
14. *(owner moved the pot 30cm)* "Someone moved me thirty centimeters. THIRTY. …I'll be fine by Thursday."

### Easygoing (pothos)
15. *(a neglected week)* "Nobody watered me all week lol. Grew anyway. We move."
16. *(a cutting was taken)* "Hey so part of me lives in Berlin now?? Little me, if you can hear this — grow loud."

### Elder (has past lives)
17. *(winter begins)* "This cold again. Osaka winters were softer, I'm just saying."
18. *(first line after handover)* "Hi. New window. I've had three before this one — the light here feels young. We'll get along."
19. *(reply: a cutting flowered abroad)* "My kid flowered in Lisbon today. …Yeah, I'm proud. Old plants get to say that."
20. *(unknown past)* "Before the shop? Nothing. Total blank. Honestly? Kind of mysterious. I like it."

## What we're measuring
- Screenshot-worthiness (still the only metric that matters at this stage)
- **Conversational feel:** does it read like talk, not captions? (new this round)
- Whether stage/temperament is *guessable* from the line (personality legibility)
- Whether any line makes you cringe (kill-list those patterns before the pilot)
