# Plant app incubator

Hobby-first web/mobile app concepts: **genuine fun, no personal-identity pressure, international from day one.**
Inspiration: [ぬい日和 Nuibiyori](https://www.nuibiyori.com/) — a closed SNS for plushie lovers where each plushie ("うちの子") has its own profile, reactions are a gentle "nadenade" instead of likes, and privacy is per post. The insight we keep: **the subject is an object, not the user**, so there is nothing to compare or judge about yourself.

## Lesson learned so far
Plant apps are a mature category — almost every single *feature* (care reminders, AI chat with your plant, avatars, timelapse, AR faces, growth prediction, cutting swaps) already exists somewhere. Originality has to come from **a moment people can't get anywhere else**, not from a new mechanic. Test each hook with: *can you say it in one sentence, and does someone react in 3 seconds?*
