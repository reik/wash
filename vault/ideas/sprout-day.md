# Sprout Day

> **Hook:** Once a year, the whole world plants together. Every plant born that day joins a **worldwide class**: same birthday, different homes, climates and hemispheres. "Class of 2027."

Back to [[index]] · Principles: [[principles]] · Sources: [[competitive-landscape]]

## The moment
- **Day 1:** opening the map and watching **tens of thousands of siblings sprout in 40 countries**.
- **Every month:** the "class photo", the same species grown wildly differently in Tokyo, Oslo and São Paulo.
- **Every year:** the class birthday.

It treats plants like children, literally: birthday, classmates, homeroom, yearbook, graduation.

## Who it's for
| Persona | Why they join |
|---|---|
| **Plant beginner** | The lowest barrier there is: one seed and a windowsill. The class gives guidance and company. |
| **Family with kids** | A shared, school-like project, and a reason to check the plant together every week. |
| **Existing plant owner** | The cutting track: root a cutting from a plant they already have. |
| **Teachers and schools** | A ready-made global classroom project (via teacher accounts; see [[principles#Privacy by default]]). |
| **Companies and teams** | Team building, an office class, CSR. |
| **Brands** | Seed companies, garden centres and eco brands as sponsors. |

## Why it's original
- **Not yet verified.** Unlike [[every-plant-has-a-past]], no competitor search has been done for this idea yet. Check citizen-science growing projects, school growing campaigns, and "grow along" challenges on TikTok and Instagram before committing. See [[competitive-landscape#Sprout Day — TODO]].
- The working assumption: synchronised one-off challenges exist, but not a **permanent global cohort** that stays together year after year.

## How it works
### The event
- **One global date**, with a 48-hour window to cover time zones. Candidate: the March equinox (around 20 March), which is spring in the north and autumn in the south. Because the tracks are indoor-friendly, the hemisphere doesn't matter.
- **Two tracks:**
  - 🌱 **Seed track**: one official species per year.
  - ✂️ **Cutting track**: root any cutting in water (pothos and similar), for people who already own plants.
- **Late bloomers:** if a seed fails, re-sow within 7 days and stay in the class. Failure must not mean being expelled from the class.

### Species choice (decide per year)
| Candidate | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Basil** (leaning) | Sold everywhere, sprouts in 5–10 days, grows indoors, edible ("first harvest" milestone) | Not very dramatic |
| Dwarf sunflower | Very dramatic, photogenic | Needs strong light; harder indoors |
| Avocado pit | Free (from the kitchen), suspenseful, globally available | Slow and unreliable (weeks to sprout) |

Rotating species gives each year's class its own identity ("Class of 2027: Basil", "Class of 2028: Sunflower").

### Structure
- **Class:** everyone born that year.
- **Homeroom:** a small international group of about 30 plants, so the community stays intimate. Members are spread across **at least 3 time-zone bands** so the chat never sleeps ([[daily-rhythm]]). (This reuses the best part of the parked pen-pal idea; see [[parking-lot]].) The homeroom is also a **plant group chat**: the plants talk to each other about real events (a new leaf, -6°C in Oslo, flowering in São Paulo) while owners read and react. See [[plant-voice]].
- **Custom groups:** a school class, office, family or city.

### Core loop between events
1. **Weekly photo day** (e.g. Sunday): a same-angle photo with the previous photo overlaid as a guide.
2. Milestones: sprouted → first true leaves → first repot → first harvest or flower → half-birthday → 1st birthday.
3. The homeroom feed with soft reactions.
4. **Class superlatives** instead of leaderboards: "tallest", "bushiest", "most dramatic comeback". Playful, never a ranking.
5. The monthly class photo (an auto-generated world collage) is the shareable artifact.

### Records
Every plant gets a Plant Record from day 1, the same model as [[every-plant-has-a-past]]. Alumni plants keep their record forever; the cohort is the start of their story.

## MVP scope
1. Pre-registration: "reserve your plant's seat in the Class of 20XX" (waitlist with a country counter)
2. Sprout Day check-in: photo of the pot or seed → joins the class and the world map
3. Weekly photo with overlay guide, and milestones
4. Homeroom of about 30 with soft reactions
5. Monthly auto-generated class photo
6. English and Japanese

**Not in v1:** custom groups (schools, companies), kits, yearbook print, the cutting track (could also launch with it if the build is cheap).

## Business model
- **Official kits** (pot + seeds + QR tag), produced and sold **locally per country** through partners. Never ship seeds across borders (biosecurity; see [[principles]]).
- **Printed class yearbook** at the 1-year mark.
- **Sponsors:** "Sprout Day 2027, presented by …" (seed companies, garden centres, eco brands).
- **B2B programs:** schools (a teacher dashboard, free or cheap, which works as marketing) and companies (paid team classes with CSR reporting).
- **Premium:** multiple plants per class, HD timelapse export, extra homerooms.

## Growth / launch
- **The press story writes itself:** "X plants born on the same day in Y countries." Prepare a press kit and a live counter.
- Pre-registration countdown with a live country map (social proof plus validation).
- Teachers and schools carry it into classrooms. Families follow.
- Each year is a new launch moment (the event recurs), and alumni bring friends into the next class.

## Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Spike then drop after the event | Homerooms, weekly photo day, monthly class photo, half-birthday; register non-Sprout plants via [[every-plant-has-a-past]] |
| Seeds fail → disappointment | 7-day late-bloomer window; pick a reliable species; cutting track as a backup |
| Kids' safety | Under-13s only through teacher or parent accounts; preset reactions only |
| Cross-border seed rules | Local sourcing only; the app never sells seeds directly across borders |
| Time zones | A 48-hour window; "born on Sprout Day" means any time inside the window |
| Originality unverified | Competitor search before any build (see Next steps) |

## Riskiest assumptions & experiments
| # | Assumption | Cheap test | Pass signal (tentative) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | People will join a synchronised planting event | "Sprout Weekend" pilot: landing page plus hashtag and Discord or Instagram; everyone plants basil on the same weekend | 300+ sign-ups from 5+ countries |
| 2 | They actually plant and post day 1 | Same pilot | More than 50% of sign-ups post a sprout photo |
| 3 | The cohort keeps them engaged | Same pilot, run 8 weeks with weekly photo prompts and small homeroom groups. Include the [[plant-voice]] test: AI-drafted plant lines that owners send, edit or keep quiet | More than 30% still posting at week 4; more than 15% at week 8; more than 60% of plant-voice drafts sent or edited |
| 4 | They pay for a kit | Pre-order page in one country | Pre-order conversion above 3% |

## Metrics
- Pre-registrations and countries represented
- Day-1 check-in rate
- Week 4 and week 8 weekly photo retention
- Kit and yearbook revenue per class member

## Open questions
- One app with [[every-plant-has-a-past]] or two brands? (Leaning: shared Plant Record backend.)
- Date: equinox versus a fixed memorable date.
- First year's species: basil versus something more dramatic.
- Homeroom size: is about 30 small enough to feel intimate?
